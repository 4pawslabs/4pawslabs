Why PixelCraft

A guide for every cell.

No drawing skill required - just patience and a grid. PixelCraft breaks any design down into simple, ordered steps so you always know exactly which cell to paint next.

Step-by-step guidance

Every design is broken into ordered steps with a live grid highlighting exactly which cells to color next, in sequential or matrix numbering.

No accounts, ever

Everything - your progress, settings, and finished pieces - stays on your device. Nothing to sign up for, nothing tied to your email or phone number.

Photograph & share

Once you've finished painting, snap a photo of your real piece and get a shareable before/after collage - straight to your camera roll or any app.

Four steps

From pick to finished piece.

1. Pick a design → 2. Set up your canvas → 3. Follow the guide, cell by cell → 4. Photograph it & share
