Why Door2Door exists

The delivery giants aren't built for your town.

They take 15-30% of every order, they own the customer relationship - and after all that, they still don't serve towns like yours. Door2Door flips the model: the shop becomes the platform, and the owner keeps the inventory, the riders, the customers, and every rupee.

0% commission

One flat monthly subscription instead of a slice of every sale. The busier your shop gets, the better the deal becomes - not the worse.

Exclusive territory

One town, one shop, guaranteed by contract. Your customers will never open this app and find a competitor - you are the platform here.

One storefront for everything

Groceries and local restaurant food, side by side. Partner shops list their menus; your desk and your riders handle the rest - and the whole town orders from you.

One app, four workspaces

Everyone in the operation, one install.

The app recognises who signed in and opens the right workspace - no separate seller app, rider app or partner dashboard to buy, learn, or keep updated.

🛒 Customers

One-tap Google Sign-In, live order tracking with a push update at every step, offers that apply themselves at checkout, home delivery or store pickup, saved addresses, one-tap reorder, ratings and a real returns flow. And the 15-minute promise: an order that isn't confirmed in 15 minutes cancels itself and tells the customer immediately - no one is ever left waiting on a silent screen.

🏪 The owner & staff

An order desk, not just an order list: an incoming queue with a countdown, where you accept, reject - or accept item-by-item when something's short. Manage the catalog, photos and bulk stock from your phone, run promotions, set per-zone delivery charges, and give staff exactly the keys their job needs: admin, accountant, warehouse.

🛵 Riders

Your own employees, not a gig fleet. Each rider sees their assigned orders and updates status with one tap from pickup to doorstep. You see every rider's live load before assigning - and a daily cash-in-hand report keeps every rupee of COD accounted for.

🍜 Partner restaurants

Local sub-shops list their menus through your storefront and get a live, view-only feed of orders containing their items. No hardware, no training - you keep coordinating with them the way you already do, just with more orders.

Placed→ Accepted→ Rider assigned→ Picked up→ Out for delivery→ Delivered

Every order moves on rails - each step recorded with who did it and when. Store-pickup orders auto-expire after 12 hours, so your shelf is never blocked by an order nobody came for.
