Who we are

A lab, not a factory.

4PawsLabs was founded by Saurav Singh Selakoti and Shubham Selakoti on a simple belief: everyday software — the app a shop runs on, the tool a family uses daily — deserves the same craft as software built for millions. So we stay small on purpose, own every layer of what we ship (design, mobile, backend, infrastructure), and measure ourselves the only way that matters: do real people use it, every day?

Flagship product

D2D — the delivery platform for small towns

The delivery giants skipped small towns. D2D turns each town's most trusted shop into its own ordering platform — customer app, owner's order desk, rider workspace and partner-restaurant view, all in one app, run by the local owner. Built end-to-end by the lab, now heading to its first town.

  • → Zero commission — a flat monthly subscription, the owner keeps every rupee
  • → Exclusive territory — one town, one shop, by contract
  • → Groceries and local restaurant food in a single storefront
  • → Live order tracking, promotions, and 30+ business reports

What we do

Two kinds of work. One standard.

Utility apps of our own

Products we conceive, design and run ourselves — apps for the errands, orders and small logistics of daily life. D2D is the first out of the lab; more are on the bench.

Client software, built to spec

You bring the requirement; we bring the whole build — mobile apps, backends, admin panels, integrations. Delivered end to end, and supported after launch, not abandoned at it.

From idea to store, fast

One small team covers product thinking, UI, mobile, APIs, data and deployment — no hand-off tax, no telephone game. Working software shows up early, then keeps showing up.

Understand→ Prototype→ Ship→ Support

That last step isn't a formality — every product we ship stays under our care, with updates and support built into how we price.
