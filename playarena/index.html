Why PlayArena

Pick it up. Play. Put it down.

No sign-up wall, no matchmaking with strangers, nothing to lose if you switch phones. PlayArena is built for the kind of games you already know, playable the moment you open the app.

No accounts, ever

Choose a name and an avatar once - both stay on your device. There's no password to forget and nothing tied to your email or phone number.

Three ways to play

Practice against a bot offline, pass the phone around the table, or generate a room code and play online with friends - every game, all three ways.

Only your friends

Online rooms are private by room code - no public matchmaking, no strangers dropped into your game unless you share the code yourself.

Six games, one app

Pick your arena.

🔢 Sudoku

Fill every row, column and box with 1-9. Solo, or race up to 4 players online.

🎱 Bingo

A 5x5 grid of 1-25. Call numbers, mark your card, complete five lines first. 2-4 players.

❌ Tic Tac Toe

The classic 3x3 grid. Play a bot or go 1v1 with a friend.

⬚ Box Bound

Draw lines, claim boxes, chain your turns. Solo, 1v1, or up to 4 players.

🎲 Ludo

Race all four tokens home around the board. 2-4 players, vs bot, pass-and-play, or online.

👑 Raja Mantri

A classic party deduction game - draw a secret role, find the Chor. 4-6 players.
