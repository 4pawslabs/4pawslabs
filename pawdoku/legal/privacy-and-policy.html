Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026 · Applies to the PawDoku Pawzzle mobile app

4PawsLabs ("we", "us", "our") operates PawDoku Pawzzle, a mobile puzzle game where you place a cat or dog companion on a grid following simple logic rules. This Policy explains what information PawDoku Pawzzle handles, and why.

PawDoku Pawzzle has no accounts. There is no sign-up, no login, no email address, and no phone number anywhere in the app.

1. Information the app stores on your device

PawDoku Pawzzle stores all of your game data locally on your own device only, using standard on-device storage. This includes:

  • Your chosen companion (cat or dog) and the name you gave them
  • Your current infinite-level progress and hearts
  • Your Daily Challenge streak, best/last solve times, and completion history (used to show your personal calendar of past days)
  • App preferences, such as sound, music, paw-hint, and notification settings
  • Whether you've purchased "Remove Ads" or a companion switch, so the app can unlock the right features on future launches

None of this is linked to your real identity, and none of it is sent to any 4PawsLabs server - we don't operate a backend for this app at all.

2. Advertising

PawDoku Pawzzle shows ads through Google AdMob, Google's mobile advertising SDK:

  • Rewarded ads - optional, and only shown when you tap a "Watch ad" button yourself, to refill hearts, recover a missed streak, or reveal a hint
  • Interstitial ads - shown at natural breaks, such as after completing certain infinite levels or a Daily Challenge

AdMob may collect data such as your advertising ID and general device information to select and measure ads, and - where you consent - to personalize them. In the European Economic Area, UK, and Switzerland, PawDoku Pawzzle shows a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) before any ad loads, and respects your choice. You can find more about how Google handles this data at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchasing "Remove Ads" (see Section 3) stops all ads in the app immediately.

3. In-app purchases

PawDoku Pawzzle offers two optional one-time purchases - "Remove Ads" and switching your companion's species - handled entirely through Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details (card number, UPI ID, etc.); Google Play processes the transaction and simply tells the app which product you bought, which we then record locally on your device as described in Section 1.

4. Sharing your results

When you tap "Share" on a solved Daily Challenge or your streak calendar, PawDoku Pawzzle generates an image entirely on your device and hands it to your phone's own share sheet. This only happens when you choose to tap "Share" yourself - the app never uploads or transmits this image anywhere on its own.

5. What we do not collect

We do not use any analytics or crash-reporting SDK beyond what AdMob and Google Play Billing include by default. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, or photos. We do not require or support account creation of any kind, and PawDoku Pawzzle has no multiplayer or chat feature.

6. App permissions

PawDoku Pawzzle requests the following device permissions, mostly included by default by the ads and billing libraries the app is built on:

  • Internet & network state - to load ads, process purchases, and fetch app updates
  • Advertising ID access (AD_ID / Ad Services) - used by AdMob to select and measure ads, as described in Section 2
  • Billing - required by Google Play Billing to process in-app purchases
  • Display over other apps / wake lock - bundled by default with the AdMob SDK to show full-screen ads and keep the app responsive while one loads

PawDoku Pawzzle does not request camera, photo library, microphone, location, or contacts access.

7. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. The only data sharing that happens is the standard exchange needed for ads (with Google AdMob) and purchases (with Google Play Billing) to function, both described above. We do not share anything with data brokers or any other third party.

8. Your rights (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023)

Because PawDoku Pawzzle does not collect or retain personal data on any 4PawsLabs server, most data-subject rights under India's DPDP Act, 2023 (access, correction, erasure) have nothing to act on - there is no stored record to look up on our end. Data handled by Google (AdMob, Play Billing) is governed by Google's own privacy policy and user controls. If you believe we hold something about you that contradicts this Policy, contact us using the details in Section 10 and we'll investigate and respond within 30 days.

9. Children's privacy

PawDoku Pawzzle does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. There is no account creation, no data collection form, and no field anywhere in the app to enter personal details.

11. Security

All network requests PawDoku Pawzzle makes (to AdMob and Google Play Billing) use encrypted connections (HTTPS). Everything else - your companion, progress, and settings - stays on your device and is never transmitted to a 4PawsLabs server.
