Why PawDoku Pawzzle

Pure logic, never luck.

Every grid is generated to have exactly one valid solution, so there's always a next deduction to make - never a coin-flip guess.

Infinite levels

Procedurally generated puzzles that grow from cozy 5x5 starter grids to tough 9x9 boards the further you climb - there's always one more level.

A new Daily Challenge

One shared puzzle every day, the same for every player. Solve it to keep your streak alive, and look back at your full history on a custom calendar.

No accounts, ever

Your companion, your progress, and your streak stay on your own device. Nothing to sign up for, nothing tied to your email or phone number.

Four rules

Everything you need to solve any grid.

1. Region constraint

Every colored region on the board must contain exactly one pet - not zero, not two.

2. Row & column control

A placed pet controls its entire row and column - those cells are locked out from every other region.

3. No touching

Pets can't touch horizontally, vertically, or diagonally - one placed pet blocks all eight surrounding cells.

4. Logic only

Every puzzle can be solved through deduction alone. No guessing - ever.
